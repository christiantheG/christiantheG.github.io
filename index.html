Research Direction

Communication Built Around Decisions.

My work connects physical-layer representation learning with resource allocation and downstream inference. The central question is simple: how can a system spend its limited communication budget on the information that changes the decision?

01

Semantic Communication

Learning compact representations that preserve task value instead of reconstructing every bit or pixel.

02

Adaptive Wireless Systems

Adapting to channel quality, symbol budgets, and Age of Information without costly online retraining.

03

Learning for Allocation

Combining reinforcement learning, optimization, and information-theoretic structure to schedule scarce resources.

Publications & Manuscripts

Research in Context.

Recent work spans significance-driven communication, pedestrian-safety monitoring, and multi-agent reinforcement learning for edge systems.

arXiv · 2026 Preprint Co-first author

Significance-Driven Semantic Communication

Christian McDowell∗, Andrea Panebianco∗, Sirin Chakraborty, and Yin Sun

Connects a decision-theoretic measure of per-sample significance with learned joint source-channel coding and communication-resource allocation. I led the design of Meta-VIB, a transceiver that adapts to SNR, codelength, and Age of Information without online retraining. The evaluated Meta-VIB/Q-Maximization design achieved up to 1,000× semantic spectrum-efficiency improvement at 0 dB and 40× at 5 dB over the tested cross-layer baselines.

An expanded journal manuscript is in preparation for submission to IEEE Transactions on Networking.

IEEE MILCOM ’26 Workshop Accepted Co-first author

SafeStep: An Interactive Demonstration of Semantic Communication for Pedestrian Safety Monitoring

Christian McDowell∗, Andrea Panebianco∗, Jeremiah Yang∗, Sirin Chakraborty, S. Chamoun, T. Ross, and Yin Sun

A live, browser-based platform for exploring how communication quality and information freshness affect pedestrian-safety inference. I am a co-primary developer of the system, which compares six semantic transceivers using four traffic-camera views.

Accepted for publication at the Quality, Value, and Age of Information for Tactical Networks Workshop at IEEE MILCOM 2026.

MAPPO for Edge Server Monitoring

S. G. Chamoun, Christian McDowell, R. M. Buchanan, K. S. Chan, E. Graves, and Y. Sun

Uses multi-agent proximal policy optimization with centralized training and decentralized execution for communication-efficient edge-server monitoring. I contributed to simulation development, experimental design, analysis, and manuscript preparation.
